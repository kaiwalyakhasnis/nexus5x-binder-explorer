Capture options

Pick a method and press Run on the phone.

The explorer will make the call on your Nexus 5X while the kernel's binder, scheduler and syscall tracepoints are recording, then rebuild every step from the app's Java code, through JNI and libbinder, into the binder driver, across to system_server, over /dev/hwbinder into the LED HAL, down to the LED driver, and back. Every value you see comes from that capture or from the source in your LineageOS tree.

Tip: start with App path → getBrightness(0). Keys: j/k or arrows step through, space plays.
